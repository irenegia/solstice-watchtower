2. What landed

Result: for a message, ok or failed with the reason (reverted by the contract, out of gas, or the call inside a Safe failed); for a claim check, whether the wallet balance moved by the event's amount; for a gap (epochs the reader could not read from the node), not read. Events have no result of their own: the result is on the message that emitted them.
